Welcome to My Investigation

The gig economy has created new ways for people to earn an income through digital platforms. Uber is one example of a platform that connects independent drivers with passengers through a mobile application.

Focus Question

How does working as an Uber driver in South Africa create employment opportunities through the gig economy, and what skills, technology and resources are needed to succeed in this career?

Focus Area

This investigation focuses on Uber driving as a career opportunity in South Africa, including the skills, technology, resources, benefits and challenges involved.

Abstract

This investigation explores how Uber creates work opportunities in South Africa through the gig economy. It examines the responsibilities of an Uber driver, the digital technology needed to perform the job, and the advantages and challenges of this type of work. Information is gathered from online research and a questionnaire to understand public opinions about Uber driving as a career.

This investigation explores how working as an Uber driver in SA creates employment opportunities through the gig economy. The findings are supported by my online research and responses from 20 questionnaire participants.

About This Investigation

This website was created as part of my Grade 11 Computer Applications Technology Practical Assessment Task (PAT) for 2026. The information presented was collected from online research and questionnaire responses.

All external information and images used in this investigation are acknowledged through source references and image captions.

Resources and Tools Used

Due to not having regular access to a computer, I initially prepared and stored my HTML code using the Notes application on my iPad. I then transferred the code to my mother's laptop using email so that I could copy and paste it into Notepad and create my website.

Other resources used during this project included Google Drive for storing my work, Google Images for relevant visual material, online research sources and Notepad for creating and editing the HTML pages.
